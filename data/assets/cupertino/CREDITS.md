# Cupertino original icons

Original first-party artwork created for Cupertino contributors, 2026.
Licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later; see LICENSE.

The SVG files are the source artwork. Run `python3 data/assets/cupertino/generate.py`
from the checkout to reproduce them and their PNG renditions with rsvg-convert.
Full-colour artwork is constructed in `artwork.py`: apps use fourth-power superellipse tiles;
folders, documents, devices, bins and overlay badges have free-standing silhouettes.
All use top-lit materials, original layered objects and separate optical editions at 16 and 32 pixels.
Symbols use a 24-unit grid, rounded terminals and a 1.9-unit regular stroke.
